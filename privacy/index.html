Privacy note

This note describes how information submitted through the Vitrus inquiry form is intended to be handled.

Information collected

The form asks for your name, email address, phone number, and consent. Company name, title, and a description of what you need help with are optional.

How it is used

The information is used only to review and respond to your inquiry, assess whether there may be a useful working fit, and continue a conversation you requested.

Storage

Responses are processed through Google Forms and stored in a private Google Sheet controlled by Vitrus. They are not sold.

Please do not submit

Do not include credentials, payment information, health information, regulated personal data, client secrets, production access details, or other confidential information in this initial inquiry.

Contact

For questions about an inquiry or to request deletion, use the Vitrus inquiry form and explain your request in the optional open text field.
